# Car Service Log

A garage-logbook app for keeping every vehicle you own organized in one
place — service history, fuel economy, running costs, documents and specs,
all synced across your devices.

Add each vehicle once, then log against it as you go. The app tracks
service intervals by km *and* date (whichever comes first) so reminders
stay accurate even for a car that's driven rarely, and keeps a running
picture of what it actually costs to own.

## What it does

- **Service history / Logbook** — log service records (oil, brakes, tyres,
  battery, air-con, general, other) with cost, odometer, workshop and
  photos; searchable and filterable by category, year or free text.
- **Maintenance schedule** — a per-item schedule (engine oil, oil filter,
  air filter, cabin filter, brake fluid, coolant and more), each with its
  own km/month interval. A "Needs attention" panel surfaces what's overdue
  or due soon, with a one-tap "Log service" action.
- **Fuel tracking** — log fill-ups (litres, price/litre, full-tank flag)
  and get fuel economy (km/L, L/100km, cost/km) calculated automatically
  between full-tank fill-ups, plus search/filter by station or tank type.
- **Expense tracking** — parking, tolls, insurance, road tax, car wash and
  anything else that isn't a service, with its own search and filters.
- **Cost Summary & Insights** — total/fuel/maintenance spend for any
  month with month-over-month comparison, plus a category/year/trend
  breakdown across service, fuel and expenses combined.
- **Car Details** — a dedicated record per vehicle for everything you'd
  otherwise have to remember or dig up: profile (VIN, trim, registration),
  tyre & wheel specs, engine & mechanical specs, documents & expiry dates
  (with attachments), parts & components, and ownership notes — each
  section edited independently.
- **Road tax & insurance** — expiry dates shown and tracked with
  overdue/due-soon status, right on the dashboard.
- **Multiple vehicles**, synced via Firebase so the same garage shows up
  on your phone and your laptop once you're signed in on both.
- **Mobile and desktop** — a floating bottom nav and full-screen forms on
  mobile, a sidebar and two-column forms on desktop.
